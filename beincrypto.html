Rebuilding a 12-language news platform from audit to design system

BeInCrypto had grown fast and the product showed it: cluttered, hard to navigate, no shared design foundations. As Design Lead I built the team, ran the research and led the redesign and the company's first design system.

The situation

When I joined, design and marketing were both being set up from scratch. There were no guidelines and no shared strategy, and the site looked like a free blog template. Organic search brought in more than half the traffic, but bounce rates sat around 75 to 80% and community and affiliate CTAs converted under 0.3%.

The content was valuable. The way it was structured and presented was the problem.

Building the team

I hired and led two teams: a product team (a UX researcher and two UX/UI designers I mentored directly) and a visual team (two graphic designers and two audiovisual designers). I also brought UX research into how product and content decisions were made, which hadn't existed before.

Getting leadership aligned

Before touching screens, I ran workshops with the executive team to agree on audience, mission and positioning, and to define personas. Leadership voted on a primary persona, a young, ambitious newcomer to crypto, which gave everyone the same lens for product, content and visual decisions.

The UX audit

I led an audit combining a multi-rater heuristic review (design, product and editorial scoring the site against eight criteria), Hotjar heatmaps and scroll maps, Google Analytics and a benchmark against Coindesk, The Block, Cointelegraph and Investopedia.

  • Readers rarely got past the first few paragraphs, and CTAs sat at the bottom where nobody saw them.
  • Site structure and visual coherence scored lowest. Multilingual consistency scored well.
  • Competitors won on scannability: short paragraphs, clear hierarchy, CTAs at natural break points.

Key decisions

Four clear entry points

Restructured the information architecture into News, Learn, Prices and Community, so newcomers had a path between news, education and tools.

Article templates built for scanning

Summaries, subheadings and scannable blocks, with CTAs moved to high-attention zones identified in the heatmaps. Ads were rebalanced to cut visual noise.

Test visual routes with real users

Two directions went to user testing. We picked the more readable, functional one over the bolder, experimental one, because trust mattered more than shock value for this audience.

A design system from day one

Typography, colour, grids, components and templates built with engineering, working across 12 languages and in light and dark mode.

Validating it

We ran prototype tests with 30 participants in Maze (80% crypto beginners), then remote moderated testing of the new site with 20 participants recruited from the community. The simplified categories and trend tags helped people explore with confidence. Trading graphs were still hard for beginners, which we flagged as the next thing to tackle.

Drop-off went from about 96% to 86%, and average time on page went from just over one minute to nearly three.

What I'd take from it

Leading design in a fast-growing company is as much facilitation as execution. The workshops and the audit gave leadership evidence to agree on, and that's what made the redesign possible.
