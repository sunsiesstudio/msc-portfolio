How I work

Product partner, not a ticket taker

Most of my recent work has been as the only designer in a trio with a PM and a dev lead. I help shape what we build and why, then I own how it works and how it ships.

Research that changes decisions

Hotelier interviews, Maze tests, Hotjar, funnel data. I use them to settle arguments and pick what to cut, then I keep measuring after launch.

Systems that flex

I built the Guest Design System so three white-label products share one foundation, while each hotel still gets its own logo, colours and type without breaking accessibility.

Building and growing teams

At BeInCrypto I hired and led a product team and a visual team from scratch, and brought UX research into how product and content decisions got made.
